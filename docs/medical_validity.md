# Medical validity

This prototype is **not** a medical device and **not** clinically validated.

- MAX30102 HR/SpO2: motion, perfusion, placement, and ambient light strongly affect readings. Single samples are rejected; windows + quality gates are required.
- MLX90614: **non-contact temperature screening**, not validated core/body temperature.
- MQ135: **relative** air indicator after warm-up, not PM2.5 or gas concentration.
- AI status is **AI-DERIVED RISK** (pattern vs personal baseline), distinct from **MEASURED SENSOR DATA**.
- Safe phrases: “Please recheck your readings.” / “Persistent abnormal pattern detected.” / “Consider appropriate medical attention if symptoms are present or readings remain abnormal.”
- Demo metrics ≠ clinical accuracy. Do not cite them as validation.
